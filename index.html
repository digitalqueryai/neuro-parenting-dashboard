Welcome 💛Read this first — the 5 basics

The most important thing to know before any activity.

Your daughter is doing brilliantly — and totally normally.
At 18 months, an attention span of 1–3 minutes per toy is exactly right. Flitting from one thing to the next is not a problem to fix — it is how a healthy toddler brain explores the world. Speaking fluent Bengali at this age shows strong, advanced language development. "Neuro-parenting" here simply means giving her brain the right ingredients: movement, the senses, language, connection and predictable rhythm. Nothing here is a test she can pass or fail.

The 5 brain-building pillars behind every activity

Move

Big movement first

High energy is fuel, not a problem. Climbing, running, dancing and carrying things builds the brain's balance, coordination and self-control systems. A child who has moved a lot can sit calmer afterwards.

📚 AAP · The Power of Play

Senses

Hands & senses

Water, mud, dough, sand, leaves. Sensory play lays down rich neural connections and is naturally calming — perfect for an energetic little one.
